Rules

Admin decisions are final.

⚔️ Versing rules (Singles)

  • You earn 1 point each time you finish ahead of your opponent in a round.
  • If another player qualifies first, the round still counts: the point goes to whichever of you and your opponent qualified first.
  • In the final round, if neither of you qualifies first, no point is awarded.
  • Once a round begins and no one has said "brb", all results stand, even if a player disconnects or their device shuts down.
  • Pushing is allowed. Teaming, or getting others to push your opponent, is not.

🚫 When a round doesn't count

  • If a player doesn't move for 10–15 seconds at the start (AFK), the round is void.
  • If a player says "brb" before the round starts, that round doesn't count unless they return within 10 seconds before the next game begins.

👥 Doubles rules

  • One set, first to 6 round wins (e.g. 6–3 or 6–4).
  • Rounds 1 and 2: both teammates must qualify. Your team wins the round if you and your teammate both qualify before both players on the other team have qualified.
    Example: you qualify, then your teammate qualifies, while only one player from the other team has made it. Your team wins the round.
  • Round 3 (final round): only 1 teammate needs to win. If you or your teammate wins the final round, your team gets the point.
    Example: your teammate wins the final round. Your team gets the point.
  • No one from either team wins the final round? If a player outside the match wins it, no one gets the point for that round.
  • All the other Versing rules, void-round rules and conduct rules apply to Doubles too.

👀 Spectating

  • Spectating is allowed, but you can't play in the same round as an active match.
  • Spectators must stay behind or camp at the start and not interfere with players competing.

⚖️ General

  • Respect all players and staff.
  • No harassment, cheating or exploiting.
  • Admin decisions are final.
  • Have fun and represent the OCE community proudly!
